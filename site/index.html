About this project

This site uses open NHS prescribing data to track ADHD medication trends over time. It started as a way to understand prescribing patterns in Northern Ireland, where diagnosis and treatment rates vary significantly between health trusts and correlate with local deprivation levels. The England dataset adds a national picture, covering over 126 months of prescribing across approximately 6,400 GP practices.

All data comes from official NHS open-data sources. Prescribing rates are expressed as items per 1,000 registered patients per month, using registered list sizes from OpenPrescribing.net. Drugs tracked include methylphenidate, lisdexamfetamine, dexamfetamine, atomoxetine, guanfacine, and modafinil — the core ADHD medication formulary used in both NI and England.

This is an independent project and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the NHS, BSO, NHSBSA, or any other public body.

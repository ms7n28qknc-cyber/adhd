    About this data

    This tool lets you explore how ADHD medication is prescribed at individual GP practice level across Northern Ireland. Use the search above to find any practice and see month-by-month prescribing trends broken down by drug.

    The data covers prescriptions issued between January 2015 and December 2025 and tracks five medications: Methylphenidate, Lisdexamfetamine, Atomoxetine, Dexamfetamine, and Guanfacine. Each record shows the number of items prescribed, quantities dispensed, and associated costs.

    Practice data is sourced from the Business Services Organisation's open data portal and is updated quarterly.

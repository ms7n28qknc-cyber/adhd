Important context — please read before interpreting these rankings.

This ranking shows the average monthly number of ADHD prescriptions issued per 1,000 registered patients at each GP practice. A higher rate does not mean a practice is over-prescribing, and a lower rate does not mean patients are being under-served.

Prescribing rates are shaped by many factors outside a practice's direct control: local demographics, the age profile of registered patients, proximity to specialist ADHD services, shared-care arrangements with secondary care, and historical referral pathways. Practices with a high proportion of young adults or those near a specialist ADHD clinic may legitimately prescribe at higher rates than the NI average.

These figures should not be used to make judgements about the quality of care provided by any practice.

Average monthly ADHD items per 1,000 registered patients — England, 2015–2025

Important context — please read before interpreting these rankings.

This ranking shows the average monthly number of ADHD prescriptions issued per 1,000 registered patients at each GP practice over the 12 months to June 2025. A higher rate does not mean a practice is over-prescribing, and a lower rate does not mean patients are being under-served.

Prescribing rates are shaped by many factors outside a practice's direct control: local demographics, age profile of registered patients, proximity to specialist ADHD services, shared-care arrangements with secondary care, and historical referral pathways. Dedicated ADHD clinics and specialist providers will appear at the very top of these rankings — that is expected and appropriate.

These figures should not be used to make judgements about the quality of care provided by any practice.
